# C-001: Cross-family top-5% consensus selects detectors with higher mean AP than model centrality on development.

status: supported (development only; bootstrap interval touches zero)
scope: development bank 65e006afd99c, 50 tasks, 20 sources, 8-candidate pool in 4 families, target ap, reference mc. Not tested on validation or test.
statement: Ranking each candidate by fractional overlap of its top-5% rows with the top 5% of the mean-rank consensus of the other families (no own-family votes, no refits) raises mean AP by +0.030 over mc and wins 13 of 20 sources. The source-level 95% interval is [-0.001, +0.066]. No gain on ROC AUC, and fixed KNN/5 still scores higher.
evidence: cab003c885da, 5173a54a6f66
