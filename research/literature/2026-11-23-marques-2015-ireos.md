# Marques, Campello, Zimek, Sander 2015: IREOS

**Source.** "On the internal evaluation of unsupervised outlier detection", SSDBM 2015. https://researchonline.jcu.edu.au/47059/

**Establishes.** IREOS scores a candidate binary top-n outlier labeling by how easily a maximum-margin classifier separates each flagged point from the rest, adjusted for chance. Only the abstract was readable; the PDF is restricted, so no numbers are verified here.

**Setting.** Unlabeled data that contains the outliers being evaluated.

**Relevance.** In our normal-only bank the flagged rows are peripheral normals, not anomalies, so IREOS measures how separable a detector's top 5% of normals is. The benchmark offers a shared-gamma IREOS via `--ireos-rows`; Ma et al. 2021 found IREOS no better than random in the contaminated setting.
