# Phan, Vu, Kieu et al. 2026: MetaEns

**Source.** "Automatic Unsupervised Ensemble Outlier Model Selection, Extended Version", arXiv 2605.16567, extending an ICML 2026 paper. https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.16567

**Establishes.** A model meta-trained on labeled datasets predicts the marginal gain of adding a detector to a partial ensemble, combined with a diversity discount and family-level risk regularization; selection is greedy with early stopping. The abstract claims consistent gains in average precision over unsupervised selectors and ensembles on 39 datasets, with no numbers. The baseline list (ELECT, MetaOD) was not verified: the full text was rate-limited.

**Setting.** Needs labeled meta-training datasets; no labels on the target.

**Relevance.** Out of scope for a single generalizing rule fitted without labeled meta-data, and meta-training on our development labels would tune to development. Its family-level risk idea, penalizing reliance on one detector family, is a usable hint.
