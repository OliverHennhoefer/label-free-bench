# Dai and Fan 2025: AutoUAD (RTM, EAG, NPD)

**Source.** "AutoUAD: Hyper-parameter Optimization for Unsupervised Anomaly Detection", ICLR 2025. https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/bf375a8dabbae40def018957ea68842a-Abstract-Conference.html

**Establishes.** Three surrogate metrics guide TPE hyperparameter search. RTM: (mean of scores above the top-5% threshold minus the median) / median. EAG: the anomaly gap averaged over thresholds from the 80th percentile to the maximum. NPD: squared mean difference between scores on diagonal-Gaussian samples N(mu_train, diag sigma²_train) and on held-out training normals, over twice the summed variances. On 38 ADBench/DAMI datasets NPD-selected OCSVM reaches mean AUC 84.03 against 74.71 for a random configuration and 85.75 for the oracle; for DeepSVDD it matches random.

**Setting.** Normal-only training (half the normals), tabular; tunes hyperparameters within one detector family, not across families.

**Relevance.** The closest published setting. RTM and EAG are in our basic bank; NPD needs `--extra-views`. Across-family selection is untested by the paper.
