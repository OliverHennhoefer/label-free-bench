# Goix 2016: excess-mass and mass-volume curves

**Source.** "How to Evaluate the Quality of Unsupervised Anomaly Detection Algorithms?", arXiv 1607.01152. https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.01152

**Establishes.** Two label-free criteria from excess-mass and mass-volume curves, estimated with uniform reference points and, in high dimension, averaged over random five-feature projections. In the novelty setting the EM ranking agrees with the ROC ranking on 28 of 36 detector pairs and with the PR ranking on 29; MV agrees on 75% and 72%.

**Setting.** Normal-only (novelty) and contaminated regimes; 12 UCI/KDD'99 datasets; only three detectors (Isolation Forest, OCSVM, LOF).

**Relevance.** The benchmark implements EM/MV on `--extra-views` banks; on the basic bank they abstain and fall back to default Isolation Forest (ledger run a1330c55e644). Agreement on 36 pairs among three detectors is weak evidence for an eight-candidate, four-family pool.
