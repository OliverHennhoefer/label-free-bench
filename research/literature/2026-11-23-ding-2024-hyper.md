# Ding, Zhao, Akoglu 2024: HYPER

**Source.** "Fast Unsupervised Deep Outlier Model Selection with Hypernetworks", KDD 2024, arXiv 2307.10529. https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.10529

**Establishes.** A hypernetwork generates autoencoder weights across hyperparameters, and a proxy validator meta-trained on labeled historical datasets ranks candidates on a new unlabeled dataset. On 35 ODDS/DAMI tabular sets its normalized ROC rank is 0.2954 against 0.3621 for ELECT and 0.3918 for MetaOD (lower is better).

**Setting.** Labeled meta-training data; deep autoencoders; contaminated targets.

**Relevance.** Like MetaOD, out of scope for a label-free rule; it shows meta-learned validators beat the earlier meta-learners, which a paper would need to cite.
