# Zhao, Rossi, Akoglu 2021: MetaOD

**Source.** "Automating Outlier Detection via Meta-Learning", arXiv 2009.10606. https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.10606

**Establishes.** Factorizes a detector-by-dataset performance matrix from labeled meta-training datasets with meta-features and a ranking loss, then predicts the best detector for a new unlabeled dataset in one shot. The abstract reports significant gains over LOF, Isolation Forest and unsupervised meta-learners; no numbers were verified.

**Setting.** Labeled meta-training datasets, tabular.

**Relevance.** Out of scope as a selector here: learning from development labels to map meta-features to picks risks recognizing datasets (hard rule 7). It remains the usual meta-learning baseline in this literature.
