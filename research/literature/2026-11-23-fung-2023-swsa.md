# Fung et al. 2023: selection with synthetic anomalies (SWSA)

**Source.** "Model Selection of Anomaly Detectors in the Absence of Labeled Validation Data", arXiv 2310.10461, v3 September 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10461

**Establishes.** From a small normal support set, build synthetic anomalies without training (CutPaste or diffusion style mixing), mix them with held-out normals and rank candidates by AUROC on that synthetic validation set. On CUB one-vs-rest it picks the best model in 109 of 200 tasks, AUROC 0.988 against 0.991 for the oracle; it is weaker on MVTec-AD and VisA.

**Setting.** Images, pretrained backbones with a kNN detector, normal-only support. The anomaly generators are image-specific.

**Relevance.** The mechanism (synthetic labeled validation from normals only) transfers to tabular data only with a tabular generator. Its weakness on industrial-defect sets suggests the result depends on how well synthetic anomalies resemble real ones, which is the risk for any tabular version.
